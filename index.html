Questions a dispatcher asks

Answered before you have to ask them on a call.

Do you need a work permit?

No. EU citizen, Bulgarian passport. No permit, no sponsorship, no visa process. Registration and tax number are handled on arrival in the country of employment.

Where would you be based?

Sofia is home, and I travel from there. For the 45 hour weekend rest I need a base or a hotel near the depot, or accommodation the company provides. Any depot with somewhere to sleep works.

Which countries have you driven?

Sweden, Denmark, the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany. Terminal work so far has been in Malmö.

Can you do rotation?

Yes. Three to six week tours, with a base or a hotel for the 45 hour weekend rest. Fixed weekly schedules work too.

Is your ADR valid for tankers?

ADR Basic and Tank, yes. Tanker experience is limited to what the licence covers, so a silo or chemical fleet should expect a short induction rather than a finished tanker driver.

What about your GDP certificate?

Completed 08 July 2026 with Leman, document PH-02-10419 v4. It is valid for one year, until 08 July 2027, so it is current now.

What are your language levels?

English at C2, Bulgarian and Macedonian native, German basic (A2). Enough for depots and paperwork with support, not enough for a German only dispatch desk.

When can you start?

Immediately. No notice period to serve.
